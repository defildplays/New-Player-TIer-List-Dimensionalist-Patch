Exactly how the score is calculated

Each chart gives points equal to its visible position: 1st = 1 point, 2nd = 2 points, and so on. Serca Gates 1 and 2 include Normal, Hard and Nightmare. Cathedral Gates 1 and 2 include Levels 1, 2 and 3. This makes 12 gate-and-difficulty combinations. Floor, Q1 and Median each add 12 item-level positions and 12 Combat Power positions: 24 positions per category, with a minimum of 24 points. Relative adds 12 positions, with a minimum of 12. Overall adds Floor + Q1 + Median + Relative: 84 positions and a minimum of 84 points. All charts count once. We do not average scores, re-rank category totals before addition, add a Relative multiplier, or weight by log count. Equal totals share the same competition rank: 1, 2, 2, 4.

Population, difficulty and patch

All modes use nDPS and the September 2026 patch. Item-level populations use the source’s full available ranges: Serca Normal 1710 ≤ ilvl < 1810, Hard 1730 ≤ ilvl < 1810, Nightmare 1740 ≤ ilvl < 1810; Cathedral Level 1 1700 ≤ ilvl < 1810, Level 2 1720 ≤ ilvl < 1810, Level 3 1750 ≤ ilvl < 1810. Every Combat Power population uses 1000 ≤ CP < 10000. Include Bus and Include Weird are off. Relative’s raid, difficulty, damage type and patch are set independently from the absolute filters. Modes are combined through rank-position points, rather than directly comparing raw damage from different encounters.

Small samples and class choice

A build is marked “Limited” if any of its 12 item-level gate-and-difficulty populations has fewer than 100 logs. This is context, not a score penalty. Adding lower difficulties brings in smaller samples for some builds, so the combined score should be read alongside the detailed evidence. Recurrence, True Courage and Judgment are damage builds; their placement does not rank support effectiveness. Raid logs do not directly measure learning difficulty, survivability or cost.

How the two population views contribute

Item-level and Combat Power charts contribute as separate lists. Their full-range populations overlap heavily, so similar source positions can contribute twice. Overall contains 24 Floor, 24 Q1, 24 Median and 12 Relative positions. Each chart has equal weight; the four categories do not have equal numbers of charts. Each raid contributes 42 positions, each gate contributes 21, and each difficulty within a gate contributes seven. In the comparison table, the displayed gate rank is calculated from that gate’s summed points; the raw points, rather than that displayed rank, feed the final score.
